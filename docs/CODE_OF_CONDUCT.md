# Ad Noctem Collective Helm Charts Code of Conduct

Ad Noctem Collective Helm Charts welcomes constructive participation from people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Contributors,
users and maintainers are expected to treat each other with respect in project discussions and collaboration.

## Our Standards

- Discuss code, ideas and behavior without attacking the person behind them.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and accept corrections in good faith.
- Respect different experience levels, communication styles and perspectives.
- Respect people's privacy, time and stated boundaries.
- Keep discussions relevant and help resolve disagreements calmly.

Technical disagreement is welcome. Direct criticism of an implementation is appropriate when it remains
respectful and explains the problem or a possible improvement.

### Unacceptable Conduct

Harassment, discriminatory insults, sexual harassment, threats, intimidation and personal attacks are not
acceptable. Neither are publishing private information without consent, repeated unwanted contact, deliberate
disruption, or retaliation against someone who raises a concern or helps investigate it.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and other project-managed spaces.
It also applies when someone represents the project publicly. Maintainers are held to the same expectations
as other participants.

## Reporting a Concern

Send conduct reports privately to the [info+charts@adnoctem.co][report_contact]. Include links or dates, a description
of what happened, any relevant context and a way to contact you. Share only the personal information needed to
understand the concern; do not publish a report containing someone else's private details in a public issue.

Maintainers will handle reports discreetly and limit sharing to people who need the information to assess or
address the situation. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed. A maintainer directly involved in a report
should step back from its assessment where another uninvolved maintainer is available.

Security vulnerabilities belong under the [Security Policy][security].

## Enforcement

Maintainers may ask participants to change their behavior, edit or remove inappropriate content, restrict
participation temporarily, or exclude someone from project spaces. The response should reflect the severity,
context and whether the behavior is repeated. Immediate restrictions may be appropriate for serious threats
or harassment.

Someone affected by a moderation decision may request reconsideration through the same private reporting
contact and provide additional context. This is a volunteer-maintained project; no fixed response deadline
is promised.
